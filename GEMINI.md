# BeliefState research

Use the connected BeliefState tools for dated investor theses, evidence-linked
risks and catalysts, changes in published views, and publication-based outcomes.
Start with free company or ticker discovery and follow the connected schemas.
Retrieve a company overview with get_brief; open a requested complete case with
get_belief. Search investment themes with search_beliefs and compare returned
case identifiers with compare_beliefs.

Preserve source URLs, attributed investors, publication and observation dates,
historical cutoffs, omissions and unknowns. Source assertions remain attributed
views. Publication-based outcomes are distinct from verified trades and do not
establish investing skill or future returns. Reuse adequate results and follow
pagination with unchanged selectors.

Research reads use the customer's BeliefState allowance or prepaid credit.
Explain access errors using the returned recovery instructions. Authenticate
through the client's OAuth flow; never ask for passwords or paste credentials
into research context. Details: https://beliefstate.ai/agent-quickstart.md.
